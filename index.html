About Us

Why Vision360?

Traditional slide-and-click H&S training has measurable completion rates but low knowledge retention. Vision360 embeds learning in context, inside the same warehouse environment trainees work in every day, so understanding follows.

🔄

360 Degree Immersive Environments

Trainees are placed inside photorealistic 360 degree warehouse scenes and asked to spot real hazards under time pressure, making learning stick through context rather than slides.

🏆

Gamified and Competitive

Scoring, leaderboards and colleague challenges transform a compliance requirement into something people actively want to return to and improve at.

📊

Instant, Meaningful Feedback

Every hazard spotted or missed is explained in plain language tied to real risk, so trainees leave with genuine understanding rather than just a pass mark.

📋

Compliance Evidence, Built In

Every attempt is timestamped and recorded. Managers can pull completion reports, scores and module history at any time, without chasing spreadsheets.

🤕

VR-Ready Architecture

Built on WebGL, scenarios run in any modern browser today and can be extended to Meta Quest VR without re-authoring content, protecting your investment.

📈

Scalable Module Library

Hazard Perception is the flagship module. The same platform and data model extend to Manual Handling, Working at Height, Unsafe Acts and Cyber Awareness without a core rebuild.

Our Mission

Replacing passive training with real engagement

Compliance training that staff actually finish and actually remember. That is what Vision360 is built to deliver, combining evidence-driven design with the kind of immersive, scenario-based learning that behavioural research consistently shows works.

🔍
Evidence-Driven Design Every design decision is grounded in usability research and heuristic evaluation against Nielsen's 10 principles
🤝
Client-First Development Built in direct consultation with Vantec to solve real operational H&S challenges, not a generic off-the-shelf solution
♿

For H&S and Operations Managers

Training your team will actually finish, and stick.

Your primary concern is not entertainment. It is demonstrable compliance. Vision360 is designed from the ground up to give you verifiable evidence that training happened, was understood, and can be reported on.

📄

Audit-Ready Completion Records

Every module attempt is automatically timestamped and stored against the trainee's account. Pull a compliance report at any point, with no manual data entry required.

✅

Proof Training Was Understood

A pass mark on a checkbox quiz tells you nothing. Vision360 records which specific hazards each trainee identified or missed, giving you genuine insight into knowledge gaps.

👥

Assign by Individual or Team

Assign modules to specific individuals, whole teams or new-starter cohorts from the admin portal. Track progress at group level without spreadsheets or manual chasing.

🔄

Retry Until Competent

Trainees who miss hazards are shown exactly what they missed and why it matters, then can retry immediately. The system records improvement over attempts, not just first-pass scores.

💻

No Installation, No IT Tickets

Vision360 runs in Chrome, Edge and Safari with no plugins, downloads or IT department involvement. Staff complete training on any shared kiosk, desktop or tablet already in the building.

🚀

Grows With Your Topic List

Hazard Perception is the first module. Manual Handling, Working at Height and Unsafe Acts follow in later iterations, all on the same platform with the same reporting infrastructure.
